MIDNIGHT STORIES — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Overview

MIDNIGHT STORIES is a horror-story app. The app does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal details.

Data and local storage

Game progress, favorites, clues, achievements, and settings may be saved locally on your device using browser storage. This information is not sent to us. Clearing the app or browser storage may erase saved progress.

Audio features

Optional narration may use the text-to-speech feature provided by your device or browser. Voice availability and processing behavior depend on the device and its system services.

Third-party services and advertising

This version does not include account registration, analytics, or advertising SDKs. If a future version adds third-party services, this policy will be updated to explain them before or when they are introduced.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Policy changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. No contact email is currently provided in the app.
